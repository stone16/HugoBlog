| BLOCKER | PLAN.md §5:111, post-migration-map.md:74,117; ADR-0002 | `translation_group_id` is an unconstrained integer with no group table, and the import contract says to assign a unique group to every post even though ADR-0002 says most posts belong to no group and groups link deliberate translated pairs. This would emit false hreflang relationships and cannot enforce the two-member zh/en invariant. | Import NULL for unpaired posts; add a translation_group table plus FK and constraints (or explicitly defer groups and omit hreflang until a pair exists). |

## WHAT I WOULD APPLY

1. Reconcile the artifact schema/handler with the spike, including immutable revisions, withdrawal, host allowlisting, and password-gate details.
2. Fix translation groups, revision deletion semantics, publish idempotency, and required indexes/retention.
3. Add explicit bootstrap, backup/restore, image migration, draft/preview, and round-trip fixture-test contracts.
4. Strengthen phase exit gates with concurrency, security, URL/redirect, and localized error tests.

## WHAT I WOULD NOT CHANGE

The Cloudflare factual constraints and the intentional cadence-risk acceptance are supported by the evidence and ADRs. The four-control security direction, D1 as canonical post storage, manual authoring, one-cutover staging strategy, CJK search spike, and social-card spike are sound decisions; they need implementation detail and tests, not another architectural reversal.
